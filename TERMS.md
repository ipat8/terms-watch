# Term Watch — Terms of Service

**Effective date: November 18, 2026**

## What Term Watch is

Term Watch is an app for Reddit's Devvit platform. Subreddit moderators install it
on their subreddit. It checks newly submitted posts and comments against a term
list fetched from a URL the moderators configure, and when a term matches, it
sends an alert to a Discord webhook the moderators configure.

## Who these terms apply to

By installing or configuring Term Watch on a subreddit, you (the moderator)
agree to these terms. Regular subreddit users don't interact with the app
directly; their public posts and comments are simply checked as described in
the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

- You must have the right to operate the webhook you configure, and your use of

- Keep your webhook URL reasonably protected: anyone who can see your
  subreddit's app settings (other moderators) or the Discord channel can see

- It does not post or comment on Reddit.
